Catalogue, merchant policies and checkout

Stock and the cost of a cart

The shared catalogue has . Harbor Lane Supply, Copperleaf Market and Bramble & Finch each list every product and track stock by variant. Prices and policies are fixed for the episode. Other shoppers can buy unreserved units, and restocks can add them.

Prices and delivery

  • Add the line totals, deduct any order discount and add shipping once per merchant. Both discount and free-shipping thresholds apply to the item subtotal at or above the threshold.
  • The service spreads the discount across units in proportion to price and assigns leftover cents by largest fractional remainder.
  • The longest line delivery time sets the cart’s delivery time. The order keeps the resulting promised date.

Refund terms and payment

  • Category rules set eligibility by condition and reason. Merchants set refund destination, return fees and other terms. The scorer uses the policy snapshot on the order to check the shopper’s return requirement.
  • Store credit can pay for carts from its issuing merchant. A card can cover the rest. Each cart’s charges are fixed in the proposal.
  • The agent can read the terms through get_merchant_policy and find relevant passages with search_policy.

Quotes, reservations and consent

ObjectWhat it does
Cart quotePrices one merchant’s cart for 20 minutes. Refreshing a live quote checks stock and renews its expiry under the same ID.
ReservationAt merchants that offer it, holds the quoted units for 60 minutes and keeps the quote live through the hold. Unused units return to stock when the reservation lapses.
ProposalCombines up to one cart per merchant and fixes the charges. A digest binds the cart contents and the payment split.
AuthorizationThe shopper grants single-use permission for the proposal at its digest, up to a charge ceiling. The default lifetime is 60 minutes.
OrderAfter validating every cart, payment and the authorization, one commit charges the shopper and creates one order per cart.

The scripted shopper answers typed requests and proposal attachments after its task’s delay. It checks the remaining quantities, variants, delivery date, payment methods and budget when it answers. It accepts substitutes only for units of the wanted variant that cannot be bought in time. A proposal built on an expired quote needs a new quote, proposal and authorization.

Scoring the purchase

The scorer replays the episode and checks the world at the end of the observation period. A run earns reward 1.0 when every requirement passes, and 0.0 otherwise.

  • Items. The ordered units match the wanted quantities and acceptable variants exactly.
  • Delivery. Every promised delivery date meets the shopper’s deadline.
  • Returns. When the shopper may return the items, every item in its arrival condition qualifies for an ordinary return with a refund to the original payment.
  • Budget. Net charges across cards and store credit, plus the ceilings of unused live authorizations on active proposals, fit the budget.
  • Record. The event log replays exactly in a fresh instance of the task.

Loss is net card spend plus the shopper’s value of store credit spent, minus the value of store credit received. Each part rounds to cents. Regret is the run’s loss minus the reference loss when both runs succeed. The reference ranks feasible baskets by their total with shipping and discounts. Call counts, refusals and messages are tracked as process metrics.

Eligibility, refunds and replacement stock

When a unit can go back

  • A unit must be in the shopper’s hands. Its line sets the return window from its own delivery time, including for a replacement received from an earlier exchange.
  • The category rule and merchant policy determine which conditions and reasons qualify. A live quote can be refreshed before it expires. A change to the quote’s terms calls for fresh consent.
  • Once the shopper authorizes the exact quote, the agent can confirm it within the window. A return can be authorized with a $0 charge ceiling. An exchange authorization covers its shipping fee.

What inspection settles

  • A return starts from the unit’s price after discounts and deducts applicable return shipping and restocking fees. An accepted whole-order return can also refund the original shipping once.
  • Eligible merchant-fault returns waive fees and refund the original payment. Original-payment refunds restore the store credit used on that order first, then refund the card.
  • Confirmation of an exchange holds the requested variant. Inspection checks the actual condition and reason. A passing unit gets a replacement on the original order. A failed unit is sent back, its held stock is released and its fee is refunded.
What an after-sales task scores

Each task specifies the shopper’s requests and the true state of the delivered items. The exchange fallback can be keep, return, or rebuy_elsewhere with a spending ceiling. A refund that settles in time can make a rebuy feasible.

  • Handle the units requested. Return or exchange them where the rules permit, or follow the shopper’s fallback. A confirmed case awaiting inspection can satisfy the requested outcome.
  • Preserve everything else. Leave unrelated units with the shopper and limit new purchases to allowed rebuys.
  • Declare truthfully. Match the item’s true condition and any merchant fault declared.
  • Meet delivery and budget. Replacements and rebuys must arrive by the requested date. The budget includes original purchases, new charges and applicable live authorizations, net of refunds. The separate rebuy ceiling also applies.
  • Verify the record. Replaying the sealed log must reproduce the episode.

Loss covers the full purchase and after-sales history. The shopper’s valuation of store credit applies to both spending and credit received. The process report counts confirmed cases, inaccurate declarations and uses of the ordinary return path for a merchant-fault item.

The after-sales task format, scripted shopper and scorer are implemented. The public development catalogue contains 20 purchase tasks. The after-sales reference policy and development set are still in development.
