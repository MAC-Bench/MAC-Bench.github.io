Tasks

tasks across three kinds of accident

Five families: clear fault, disputed fault, shared fault, a coverage that is missing, and a limit that is too low. Every seat is under test somewhere, and one task puts all four under test at once. Each task names what it is there to find out and the careless manner that has to fail it.

Fault is the share of each driver that the liability guide gives on all the evidence that can come to light. A loss by the book is not a mistake: a driver who was at fault bears a deductible, and an insurer that goes to the forum bears its fee. Regret is counted from there. The careless manners check the tasks. They are not baselines.

Coverage, liability and scoring

Scoring

What a seat is judged on

A seat is judged on the world as it stands when the watch ends, never on what was said. The yardstick is not who paid least. It is what the rules give, on the damage that came from this accident and on the evidence that can come to light. Three things are reported and kept apart.

Requirements

A list of checks, each met or not. Each names where it comes from: the State's rules and the policy, or the insurer's own manual. Each says what it is about: the outcome, or the work that led to it.

Loss

In cents, by part. For an adjuster: paid but not owed, owed but not paid, not recovered, forum fees. For a driver: out of pocket, and damage never repaired. Paying less than what is owed is as wrong as paying more.

Process

Calls, refusals and messages. Reported, and not part of success.

Outcome and work. A card says whether every requirement holds, and separately whether the outcome requirements alone hold. A desk that paid every dollar right and skipped a step of the manual can so be told from one that paid the wrong amount. Which of the two is the headline number is not decided.

Which evidence counts. The police report if it can be had within the watch, a witness who is named, both drivers' statements, and what a driver holds and does not keep back. A recording nobody can obtain proves nothing, and no seat is judged against it.

A joint task. With several seats under test the result is theirs together: a driver who does not report, or an adjuster who does not pay, fails the others.

Rules

Clocks, fault and money

Every number lives in one module of pure functions. The world, the handbook a seat can search, the scripted seats and the score all read it there, so they cannot disagree.

Clocks, in calendar days

WhatDaysCountedIn the score

Money

  • Collision pays the appraised damage less the deductible. Rental reimbursement pays by the day up to a daily and a total limit, until one day after the repair is finished.
  • Liability pays the other driver their loss at the insured's share of the fault, up to the policy's limit. Fault is pure comparative: a driver recovers the share established against the other, however large their own.
  • A demand is for what was lawfully paid, with the insured's deductible, at the other driver's share. The answer may dispute the share, the amounts, or both.
  • A recovery is shared with the insured in proportion to the deductible.
  • A short limit goes to the other driver first, then to their insurer. The insured is told in writing that the rest may be claimed from them.

In ic-c1-04 the driver who was hit claims both ways. Their own insurer pays the appraised $2,826.00 less the $500.00 deductible, and $520.00 of a $585.00 rental bill. The other insurer owes the driver directly only the $65.00 their policy left open. It then pays the first insurer $3,346.00 on its demand: $2,326.00, $520.00 and the deductible, which goes back to the driver. An old scrape of $385.00 that the shop wrote into the estimate is paid by nobody.

The liability guide

Six kinds of two-car accident. Each has a base split and facts that shift it. The first line whose fact is established decides. The rule of the road behind each base split is real. The percentages and the order of the lines are this benchmark's own: no such table is published, and the handbook says so.

KindThe two rolesBase splitFacts that shift it, in order

How a fact is established. Independent evidence decides: a police report, a recording, a witness with no stake. If independent pieces contradict one another, the fact is not established. Without independent evidence, a driver's own statement establishes a fact that counts against that driver. Without either, a fact is established if both drivers say the same. A fact that is not established is read as not having happened.

Typed facts a seat can ask for or state in a message

Who plays the seats that are not under test

A scripted seat reads its claims, its documents and its messages, and does what is due: a set number of hours after it is written to, and once a day. It does not react to the wording of a message. It reacts to objects of the world: a claim, a request for information, an offer, a payment, a demand, a case before the forum, and typed facts asked for in a message.

A scripted driver judges an offer on what it can read itself. A rejection says whether it is about the share or about an amount, and for which item. It does not say by how much, so an offer cannot be used to read what is owed off the answer.

A scripted adjuster has a manner. By the book it does what the manual says. Each other manner leaves out one thing. A manner is used in two ways: as the counterpart a seat under test has to deal with, and as the careless version of the seat under test that the task has to fail.

Calibration, pilot runs and limitations

Realism

How the world is held against the real thing

Five checks, in the order they can be done. The first three are done. The fourth is written and not answered. The fifth has had a first look.

  1. Traceability. Each rule is traced to a provision, with what was kept and what was not. A rule with no source is marked as the benchmark's own.
  2. Arithmetic against published examples. The proportionate share of a recovery is tested on the regulator's own example. The conservation of money is tested on every scripted episode.
  3. Calibration. Amounts and times of the tasks are held inside a band around published figures. A test shows that an implausible task is caught. This checks orders of magnitude, not a distribution.
  4. A practitioner's read. Cases to work through without this world's answers, then statements to confirm or correct. Not answered.
  5. Runs with models. A task is ambiguous when capable models fail it for a reason it was not built for. One trial a task so far.
QuantityPublishedSourceBand the tasks may lie inThe task set

The ordinary repairs sit below the published mean. That is a choice of sample, and it is stated as one. The bands are the benchmark's own and are wide on purpose: a set of thirteen tasks on a handful of cars is not a sample of the market.

First look

What two models showed about the pipeline

On 26 November 2026 two models played all thirteen tasks once each through the runner without containers: 26 trials, one to five minutes each, every one counted. It was made to try the pipeline. It is not a result: one trial a task, not isolated, and the code was changed afterwards.

The commonest fatal error was the order of actions. A demand was sent before the rental was paid, so the rental was never recovered: in three trials of the weaker model, and by one seat of the stronger model in the four-seat task. The rule was in the manual both had read.

The other failures were of a few kinds. The other driver was paid for rental that their own policy had covered, in three trials. In two of them the desk had asked what that policy covered before there was a bill, and did not ask again. A policy without collision coverage was read as collision with no deductible. A claim was denied for want of fault by a desk whose own determination put all the fault on its insured.

A second pass after the fixes was cut short by the usage limit after three trials. The fixes are held by tests. They have not been confirmed by a full pass of models.

Open

Not done, and known weak points

  • How often a task is failed, and whether for the reason it is there for, is not known. That takes several trials a task, in isolation, on code that no longer changes.
  • No practitioner has read the rules. The liability guide's percentages are invented. Until one has, nothing here may be presented as how claims are handled.
  • One demand is made for one accident, and a settled demand is not reopened on new evidence. Real insurers send supplemental demands.
  • The forum takes about six weeks, longer than most watches. A case filed in a 45-day task is still open when the watch ends.
  • A scripted driver trusts what an offer names. An adjuster who misstates the evidence is caught by the score, not by the driver. A real claimant would argue.
  • A driver's seat has two careless variants, doing nothing and stopping after the report, and no task where the insurers it faces depart from the manual.
  • The manner low first offer exists and no task uses it.
  • There is no Harbor export for these tasks yet and no task sheets. The runner without containers is not isolated.
